Pathfinder Pad

Carry the light through the dark. A maze game for iPad and Apple Pencil: 60 levels across six regions, plus online duels for 2–4 players.

How to play

  • Put your pencil on the glowing light and guide it to the green exit without touching the walls.
  • In the dark, tilt the pencil to widen your lantern.
  • Squeeze Apple Pencil Pro to fire your one flare per level. From level 6 you choose its colour: red is wide but brief, amber balanced, green shows the way, blue is narrow but long, violet reveals secret walls.
  • Press hard to open orange pressure doors. Double-tap to return to your last checkpoint.
  • Collect every crystal for the third star.

Frequently asked

Do I need Apple Pencil?

Apple Pencil gives the best experience, and Apple Pencil Pro adds squeeze and haptics. You can also play with your finger: tap the pencil button in the lower-right corner to open the virtual pencil, where you can squeeze, double-tap and set pressure and tilt.

How do duels work?

Open Duel from the home screen and sign in to Game Center. Find a match or invite friends; everyone races through the same maze. No internet? Choose “Practise vs. bot”.

Is my progress backed up?

Progress is stored only on your iPad. See the Privacy Policy.
