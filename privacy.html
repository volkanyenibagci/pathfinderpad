Pathfinder Pad

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

The short version

Pathfinder Pad does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPad

Your level progress, stars and settings (sound, haptics, flare colour) are saved only on your device. They never leave it and we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Online duels and Game Center

The optional Duel mode uses Apple’s Game Center to find opponents and to exchange live race data (your position in the maze, crystals and flares) with the other players in the same match. This data is sent through Apple and is used only during the match; we do not store it. Your Game Center name is shown to the players you race against. Game Center is provided by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

Because we collect no personal data, Pathfinder Pad collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
